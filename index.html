Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is Famspot free?

Yes. Famspot is free to download and use.

Which areas does it cover?

We're starting in the Quad Cities area. Enter any ZIP code to see events within your chosen radius.

Which devices are supported?

Famspot is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Can I add an event?

Yes — sign in and tap Post to share a family-friendly event or class with other local parents.

Can you bring Famspot to my area?

We're live in the Quad Cities now, with more areas coming soon. Email us at support@famspotapp.com and tell us your ZIP code.

How is my data used?

We don't sell your data or use it for third-party advertising profiles. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from within the app's Profile screen.
